# Post-Task Survey Instrument

Use this after each condition run.
Scale for all closed items:

1 = Strongly disagree
2 = Disagree
3 = Slightly disagree
4 = Neutral
5 = Slightly agree
6 = Agree
7 = Strongly agree

Record metadata:

- Group ID:
- Participant ID:
- Condition: `AI-on` or `AI-light`
- Order: `AB` or `BA`
- Run number: `1` or `2`
- Scenario variant: `A` or `B`

## Section A: Shared Understanding

1. Our team quickly aligned on a clear objective.
2. Project context updates helped us stay aligned.
3. We had minimal confusion about goals and scope.

## Section B: Coordination Quality

4. Messages were clear and actionable.
5. Handoffs between teammates were smooth.
6. We spent less time in repetitive back-and-forth than usual.

## Section C: AI Routing Fit

7. We could tell when Ask was appropriate.
8. We could tell when Research was appropriate.
9. AI responses matched the intent of our prompts.
10. I trusted the AI outputs enough to use them in decisions.

## Section D: Decision Workflow Fit

11. Accept/dismiss action flows matched our real teamwork behavior.
12. Marking actions complete felt natural and useful.
13. The action workflow helped us reach decisions faster.

## Section E: Traceability and Verification

14. It was easy to trace decisions back to source context.
15. Marker-to-insight navigation improved confidence in decisions.
16. I could verify why a recommendation was made.

## Section F: Outcome and Usability

17. We finished with a usable deliverable.
18. The final output quality was better than our typical baseline.
19. The tool improved overall team productivity for this task.
20. I would use this workflow again for real team work.

## Section G: Workload (Lightweight)

21. The workflow felt mentally manageable under time pressure.
22. Tool interactions did not distract from task completion.

## Section H: Open Responses

1. What helped your team most in this condition?
2. What slowed your team down most in this condition?
3. Where did AI help, and where did it fail?
4. What would you change in this workflow?

## Peer Rating Add-On (Optional but Recommended)

For each teammate, rate 1-7:

1. Communication clarity.
2. Contribution to decisions.
3. Effective use of AI interactions.

Use participant codes only (no names).

## Validated Instrument Add-Ons (Recommended)

Use official validated forms from your institution/library sources.
Do not rewrite item wording unless method guidelines allow it.

Recommended options:

1. `UMUX-Lite` (Lewis et al.) for compact usability measurement.
2. `SUS` (Brooke) if you want the classic 10-item usability benchmark.
3. `NASA-TLX` (Hart and Staveland) for perceived workload.
4. `Trust in Automation` (Jian, Bisantz, Drury) for trust calibration.

Suggested minimal set for this study:

1. UMUX-Lite
2. Trust in Automation

Suggested expanded set:

1. UMUX-Lite or SUS
2. Trust in Automation
3. NASA-TLX

Scoring capture fields to add in analysis sheet:

- UMUX-Lite score (0-100 transformed)
- SUS score (0-100) if used
- NASA-TLX raw average (0-100)
- Trust scale total and subscale means (if defined)
